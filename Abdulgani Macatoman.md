Abdulgani Macatoman

Public Servant | Entrepreneur | Author | TV Host | Rider | Traveler | Extreme Sports Enthusiast | Reservist Colonel

Abdulgani Macatoman is a Filipino public servant, entrepreneur, author, and television host with more than 30 years of leadership experience spanning government, business, and socio-civic service. He previously served as Undersecretary of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and Acting Secretary of the Commission on Filipinos Overseas (CFO), where he championed investment promotion, MSME development, and inclusive economic growth.

He is a business consultant, independent corporate director, and advocate for entrepreneurship, peace-building, and sustainable development, particularly in Mindanao. As host of Kabuhayan at Negosyo and author of Global Halal Economy, he continues to promote innovation, investment, and opportunities for Filipino entrepreneurs.

He earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (Major in Accounting) from the University of the East, pursued a Bachelor of Laws, and completed a Master of Business Administration (MBA). Beyond his professional life, he is an avid rider, traveler, extreme sports enthusiast, and serves as a Reservist Colonel, reflecting his lifelong commitment to leadership, service, and nation-building.
